Yeet It! Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Yeet It! (the “app” or the “game”), an iPhone game published by Narek Nikoghosyan (“we”, “us”). It covers the game itself, its anonymous gameplay statistics and the advertising inside it.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no sign-up and no chat. We never ask for your name, email or any other personal details.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers and we receive none of this data.
  • Only if you turn them on in the game, the game sends anonymous gameplay statistics (for example, how many attempts a level took) to Yandex AppMetrica, so we can see which levels are too hard. They are off until you agree, contain no personal details, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings → Statistics.
  • The game shows ads from the Yandex Advertising Network. Yandex may collect device and usage data to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • You control ad tracking with Apple’s tracking prompt.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally on your iPhone using Apple’s standard app storage (UserDefaults):

  • your best star rating for each level, how many times you have started each level, and whether you have finished the tutorial;
  • your sound, haptics (vibration), reminder and statistics settings.

This information stays on your device and contains nothing that identifies you. The only thing the game itself sends anywhere is the anonymous gameplay statistics described in section 3, and only if you have turned them on. The game has no crash reporting or tracking of its own.

You can erase your progress at any time with Settings → Reset progress in the main menu. Deleting the app removes all of the information above from your device. Depending on your iOS backup settings, it may also be included in your device backups (for example, iCloud Backup), which are handled by Apple.

2. Advertising (Yandex Advertising Network)

The game is free and is supported by ads from the Yandex Advertising Network, through the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (provided by YANDEX LLC). The SDK prepares ads in the background and the game shows one when it is due. There are two kinds of ads:

  • Ads between levels (full-screen) that may appear when you move on after completing a level;
  • Optional video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for a Hint (showing the full trajectory of your next throw) or an extra throw.

Yandex acts as an independent provider of these ads. When ads are requested, shown or tapped, the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK (which includes components of Yandex’s AppMetrica SDK for measurement) may collect and process information such as:

Type of informationExamples
Device identifiersthe Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see section 5), and other identifiers such as the identifier for vendors (IDFV) or an identifier generated by the SDK
Approximate locationa coarse location estimated from your IP address (not precise GPS location; the game does not request location access)
Advertising datawhich ads were shown, and whether they were viewed or tapped
Product interactionapp launches and interactions with ads
Diagnosticscrash data, performance data and other technical information about the device, operating system and app version

Yandex uses this information to deliver ads, measure their performance, limit how often you see the same ad, prevent fraud and abuse and, where you have allowed it, to show personalised ads. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you; we only see aggregated reports in our Yandex Advertising Network account.

To learn how Yandex handles this information, see the Yandex Privacy Policy and the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK Terms of Use.

Ads may also use Apple’s SKAdNetwork, a privacy-preserving system that lets advertisers measure app installs without identifying you.

3. Gameplay statistics (Yandex AppMetrica)

To find out which levels are too hard and where players get stuck, so that we can reorder or fix them, the game can send short anonymous gameplay events — only if you agree (see “Your choice” below) — to AppMetrica, an app analytics service provided by YANDEX LLC:

  • which level was started, cleared, failed (out of throws) or left from the pause menu, the attempt number on that level, the number of throws and stars, how long the attempt took, and whether a Hint or an extra throw was used;
  • which tutorial steps were shown, and whether the tutorial was finished or skipped;
  • the language of the game’s interface (English or Russian).

These events contain no name, email, account, precise location or other personal details, and the game does not use them for advertising or to track you. Like any analytics service, AppMetrica receives them together with technical information about the device and the app (such as the device model, operating system and app version, an identifier generated by AppMetrica, and an approximate country estimated from your IP address). We see only aggregated reports in our AppMetrica account — for example, the median number of attempts per level. Yandex processes this information under the Yandex Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use of Yandex Metrica and AppMetrica.

Your choice. The statistics are off by default, in every country. After you clear your first level, the game asks once, in its own window, whether you would like to share them; they are sent only if you tap Sure, or if you later turn on Settings → Statistics in the game’s main menu. If you tap No thanks, the game does not ask again. Until you agree, no events are sent or kept for later. The statistics are available only in the countries listed in section 4. You can stop them at any time by turning off Settings → Statistics; sending stops immediately.

4. Where the game is available

The game is released only in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Armenia and Azerbaijan. In these countries (determined from your App Store country or device region), ads may be personalised if you allow tracking (section 5), and you can choose to share gameplay statistics (section 3).

If the game is used anywhere else, it tells Yandex that no consent has been given, so ads are shown without personalisation and without the tracking prompt, and gameplay statistics are not sent, even if Settings → Statistics is on.

5. App Tracking Transparency

iOS shows its standard prompt asking whether Yeet It! may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites.

  • If you allow tracking, Yandex may access your device’s Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and use it to show more relevant ads and to measure them.
  • If you ask the app not to track, the Advertising Identifier is not available to the app or to Yandex. You will still see ads, but they will not be personalised using the IDFA. The game works exactly the same either way.

You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or in iOS Settings → Yeet It! (“Allow Tracking”).

6. In-app purchase

Yeet It! currently has no in-app purchases.

7. Children

Yeet It! is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no accounts, chat or other way to submit personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and have questions about how ads in the game handle data, please contact us using the details below. You can also restrict tracking on a child’s device using Screen Time and the iOS tracking settings described above.

8. Data retention

  • On your device: progress and settings are kept until you reset progress or delete the app.
  • With us: we do not collect or store any personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain. Anonymous gameplay statistics are kept in our AppMetrica account as aggregated reports and raw anonymous events for as long as AppMetrica stores them.
  • With Yandex AppMetrica: gameplay statistics are retained according to the Yandex Privacy Policy and AppMetrica’s data retention terms.
  • With Yandex: data collected by the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK is retained according to the Yandex Privacy Policy.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Because we do not hold personal data about you, the most effective ways to exercise these rights are:

  • use the iOS Tracking setting;
  • turn gameplay statistics on or off at any time with Settings → Statistics in the game’s main menu;
  • reset progress in the game or delete the app to remove on-device data;
  • contact Yandex for data processed by it, as described in the Yandex Privacy Policy.

You are also welcome to contact us with any request or question, and we will help as far as we can.

10. International processing

Yandex (including AppMetrica) and Apple may process information on servers located outside your country, including in Russia (Yandex) and the United States (Apple), under their own safeguards for international data transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game’s handling of information changes (for example, if a new advertising provider is added), we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Significant changes will be reflected in the app’s App Store privacy information.
